## Product

**Name:** Veld Portfolio  
**URL:** veldportfolio.com  
**Category:** Real estate portfolio analytics and deal underwriting SaaS  
**Stage:** Early (active users, real revenue, growing)

### What it does

Veld is an all-in-one portfolio tracking and deal analysis platform for small rental property investors. Users add their properties once and get:

- **Portfolio dashboard** — equity, cash flow, cap rate, LTV, and rent vs. market benchmarks across all properties, always current
- **Property detail** — per-property metrics, mortgage amortization, scenario projections (5/10/20 year hold, rent growth, expense assumptions, optional sale)
- **Deal analyzer** — full acquisition underwriting: cash flow, cap rate, DSCR, cash-on-cash return, GRM. Save analyses, compare multiple deals, promote a winning deal to the portfolio when you close (assumptions carry over)
- **Mortgage simulator** — amortization tracking and payoff modeling per property
- **Scenario modeling** — what-if sliders for rent, value, and mortgage assumptions on owned properties
- **Rent & value estimates** — live market data via RentCast, shown alongside user-entered assumptions so they can see if they're above or below market without switching tabs
- **Calculators** — free public calculators (BRRRR, Fix & Flip, STR vs LTR, Deal Analyzer) usable without an account
- **CSV import/export** — import from existing spreadsheets using documented column format; export for accountant/tax use

### What it does NOT do

Veld is intentionally scoped. Be explicit about this — honesty builds trust with this audience:
- No rent collection or payment processing
- No bank sync or transaction import
- No full general-ledger accounting
- No tenant screening or lease management
- No property management workflows

It works alongside whatever banking, PM, or accounting tools users already have.

## Target Audience

### Primary persona: The small passive landlord

- Owns **1–10 rental properties** (sweet spot: 1–5)
- Does **not** actively manage rentals day-to-day — has a property manager, or properties that run themselves
- Thinks of themselves as an **investor**, not a landlord-as-job
- Holds properties for appreciation, cash flow, or both — not a flipper
- Frustrated that **real estate tools are fragmented** across multiple websites
- Currently uses a **spreadsheet** to track their portfolio — and hates it (broken formulas, version chaos, re-entering data)
- Does not want complexity — wants the numbers to be right without maintenance
- Evaluates new acquisitions before buying but often doesn't have a structured workflow for it

### Secondary persona: The aspiring investor

- Does not yet own property but is actively evaluating deals
- Uses the free calculators before signing up
- Converts when they close their first deal and need a place to track it

### Who it is NOT for

- Full-time property managers with large portfolios (20+ properties, multiple staff)
- Commercial real estate investors
- Institutional buyers
- House flippers (short hold periods, not portfolio tracking)
- People who need accounting or rent collection in the same tool

## Core Positioning

### Single sentence
One place to track your rental portfolio and analyze new deals — without re-entering data everywhere or maintaining spreadsheets.

### The origin story (authentic, use this)
Built because the founder was annoyed that real estate tools were fragmented across different websites. Every tool required re-entering the same property data. Spreadsheets were error-prone. There was no single place to both track an existing portfolio and run deal analysis on potential acquisitions.

### Key differentiators vs. spreadsheets
1. Data entered once — no re-entering the same numbers in different sheets
2. Consistent metric definitions (cap rate, DSCR, cash flow) — no formula drift
3. Deal analysis alongside portfolio tracking in one product
4. Live rent/value market benchmarks without switching tools

### Key differentiators vs. Stessa / Rentastic (accounting-focused tools)
1. Dedicated deal underwriting workspace (competitors lack this or have a limited version)
2. Scenario modeling on owned properties (what does this property do in year 10?)
3. Mortgage amortization and payoff tracking per loan
4. No bank sync required — add your numbers manually without connecting accounts
5. Explicit about what it doesn't do — no accounting, no rent collection — so users can pick the right combination of tools

### Key differentiators vs. Cozy (legacy, shutdown)
Cozy was acquired and changed course. Landlords who used it for tracking need an analytics layer. Veld fills that role and adds deal underwriting they didn't have before.

## Pricing

All tiers include: deal analyzer, scenario modeling, mortgage simulator, portfolio charts, rent & value estimates, public calculators.

| Tier | Monthly | Annual | Properties | Saved Deals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | 1 | 5 |
| **Investor** | $15/mo | $150/yr (2 months free) | 5 | 20 |
| **Pro** | $29/mo | $290/yr (2 months free) | 20 | 50 |

**Free tier philosophy:** The free tier is the front door. It's generous enough to prove value (full feature access, just limited properties/deals) and converts users who have grown their portfolio. No credit card required. No time limit.

**Upgrade trigger:** Free users upgrade when they hit the 1-property limit (most common) or the 5-deal limit for analysts who save a lot of comparisons.

**Estimate limits (RentCast API):** Free: 5/hr, Investor: 10/hr, Pro: 20/hr. Shared pool per user.

## Funnel Structure

### Acquisition sources
- Organic SEO (calculator pages, competitor alternative pages, blog/changelog)
- Reddit (r/realestateinvesting, r/landlord, r/buildinpublic)
- Google Ads (search — "portfolio tracker intent" angle is the proven winner; see below)
- Direct/word of mouth

### Google Ads — what's working (as of April 2026)

**Campaign:** "Search | Portfolio Tracker Intent" — targets users actively searching for rental property portfolio tracking tools.

**Performance (Apr 12–16, 2026, $35/day budget):**
- 743 impressions, 30 clicks, 4.04% CTR
- $0.66 avg CPC, $19.89 total spend
- 3 conversions at 10% conv. rate — **$6.63 cost per conversion**

**Why this angle wins:** People searching for a portfolio tracker have immediate intent — they already own properties and are frustrated with their current setup (usually a spreadsheet). This matches the primary persona exactly. They convert at 10% vs. typical SaaS 2–5%.

**Budget:** Increasing from $35 → ~$60–70/day while still in learning mode (campaign needs ~50 conversions to exit learning). Scale further once learning mode exits and conv. rate holds.

**Do not confuse this angle with deal-analysis intent** (BRRRR, fix-and-flip, DSCR searches) — those attract the aspiring/analyst persona who may not yet own properties. Portfolio tracker intent = existing owners ready to replace their spreadsheet.

### Top-of-funnel assets (free tools)
Public calculators drive SEO traffic from people actively evaluating deals:
- `/investment-property-calculator` — Deal analyzer (primary, most traffic)
- `/tools/brrr` — BRRRR method
- `/tools/fix-and-flip` — Fix and flip analysis
- `/tools/str-vs-ltr` — Short-term vs long-term rental comparison

These convert calculator users → signed-up users at higher rates than generic landing visitors.

### Competitor pages (high-intent SEO)
- `/stessa-alternative` — vs Stessa
- `/rentastic-alternative` — vs Rentastic
- `/cozy-alternative` — vs Cozy (legacy)
- `/vs/spreadsheets` — vs spreadsheets
- `/vs/excel-rental-property` — vs Excel trackers

### Conversion path
Landing / calculator / competitor page → Sign up (free, no card) → Add first property → See dashboard → Hit property limit → Upgrade to Investor
