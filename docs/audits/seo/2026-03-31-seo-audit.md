# SEO Audit — 2026-03-31

**Product:** Veld Portfolio (Next.js app under `app/`).  
**Process:** [docs/process/seo-audit-process.md](../../process/seo-audit-process.md)  
**Scope:** Audit-only; no code changes.

---

## Executive summary

- **Overall:** Crawl signals (**`robots.ts`**, **`sitemap.ts`**) align with the intended **indexable marketing surface**: hub, calculators, pricing, changelog, and legal. **`proxy.ts`** public allowlist matches that surface plus **noindex** routes (`/contact`, `/lp/investment-property-calculator`, auth). Canonicals and **`metadataBase`** use **`NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL`** with fallback `https://veldportfolio.com`.
- **Top risks:** (1) **Root `title.template`** (`%s | Veld Portfolio`) likely **doubles the brand** on pages whose `title` already ends with “| Veld Portfolio” or embeds the full brand string (e.g. **home**, **changelog**, **contact**), hurting length and SERP clarity. (2) **`robots.txt`** does not list several **auth-gated** paths (`/plans`, `/calculators`, `/mortgage`, etc.); crawlers rely on redirects/noindex on individual pages—**weaker** than explicit `Disallow` for defense in depth. (3) **BRRRR** public calculator has **no FAQ JSON-LD** while other calculators do—**inconsistent** rich-result eligibility.
- **Recommendation:** Fix **title/template** usage (e.g. `title.absolute` or shorten child titles) as the highest-impact metadata fix; tighten **`robots.ts`** for non-public path prefixes; align **JSON-LD** across calculator templates where FAQs exist on-page.

## Severity-ranked findings

### Critical

- None identified in static review (assuming production serves **`/og-image.png`** and **`/logo.png`** — both present under `app/public/` in workspace).

### High

- **Title template vs page titles** — Redundant “| Veld Portfolio” (or equivalent) when combined with `layout.tsx` **`title.template`**, producing over-long or repetitive `<title>` values on **home**, **changelog**, **contact**, and any page embedding the full brand in `title`. — `app/app/layout.tsx`, `app/app/page.tsx`, `app/app/changelog/page.tsx`, `app/app/contact/page.tsx`

### Medium

- **`robots.ts` coverage** — Auth-only areas (`/plans`, `/calculators`, `/mortgage`, `/modeling`, `/export`, etc.) are not **`Disallow`**’d; reliance on Clerk **`auth.protect()`** behavior for bots is implicit. — `app/app/robots.ts`, `app/proxy.ts`
- **BRRRR calculator** — No **FAQPage** JSON-LD while **STR vs LTR**, **fix-and-flip**, and **investment property** calculators include it; inconsistent eligibility for FAQ rich results. — `app/app/tools/brrr/page.tsx`
- **Terms meta description** — Very short; may underperform in snippets vs privacy. — `app/app/terms/page.tsx`

### Low

- **Changelog `keywords`** — Marginal SEO value; could be removed without loss. — `app/app/changelog/page.tsx`
- **Sign-in / sign-up** — Only **`robots`** set; titles fall back to layout default — acceptable for **noindex** but tab titles are generic if bookmarked. — `app/app/sign-in/[[...sign-in]]/page.tsx`, `app/app/sign-up/[[...sign-up]]/page.tsx`

## Risk & impact assessment

Unresolved **title/template** issues most directly affect **CTR and brand clarity** in SERPs and social tab titles, not crawlability. Thin **robots.txt** coverage for gated paths is **low probability** exposure if auth always returns non-200 HTML to unknown crawlers, but **explicit disallow** reduces ambiguity. Missing BRRRR FAQ schema is **incremental** rich-result opportunity only.

---

## Recommendations (prioritized)

1. **Normalize titles** against `layout.tsx` **`title.template`**: use short leaf titles (e.g. “Pricing”, “Changelog”) or **`title: { absolute: "…" }`** where a full custom string is required — fix home, changelog, and contact first.
2. **Extend `robots.ts` `disallow`** with path prefixes for authenticated app sections (`/plans`, `/calculators`, `/mortgage`, `/modeling`, `/export`, `/dashboard`, etc.) to match **`proxy.ts`** intent and reduce accidental crawl budget use.
3. **Add FAQ JSON-LD to BRRRR** if on-page FAQ-style blocks exist or are added; otherwise document intentional omission.
