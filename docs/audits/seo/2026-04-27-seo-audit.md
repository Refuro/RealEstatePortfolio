# SEO (Search & Discovery) Audit — 2026-04-27

## Executive summary

- **Overall health:** Strong. `robots.ts`, `sitemap.ts`, and `getAppOrigin()`-based canonicals form a consistent crawl and discovery story; the authenticated app shell is `noindex` at `app/app/(app)/layout.tsx`, and `proxy.ts` public-route allowlists align with the marketing surfaces that should be reachable without sign-in. Apex canonical hosting is reinforced by a permanent `www` → `veldportfolio.com` redirect in `app/next.config.ts`.
- **Top risks:** (1) **Programmatic scale and overlap** between the national investment-property calculator and the state-level `/tools/investment-property/{state}` program — not a canonical bug, but worth monitoring in Search Console for near-duplicate or clustering behavior. (2) **Environment-dependent** absolute URLs: `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` must be correct on every deploy surface so sitemaps, robots, and metadata do not point at the wrong host. (3) **Production-only** verification (HTTP status, URL Inspection, sign-off on staging noindex) cannot be completed from the repo alone; those checks remain a follow-up.
- **Recommendation:** Treat SEO maintenance as a quarterly review: confirm Search Console coverage for the large calculator grid, keep `llms.txt` in mind for AI/answer-engine discovery, and address only high-confidence gaps in internal linking and share-preview differentiation where the business cares about social or referral.

## Severity-ranked findings

### Critical

- None. Authenticated and transactional routes are not intended to rank; they combine `robots` path disallows, shell-level `noindex` where appropriate, and Clerk protection for non–public paths (`app/proxy.ts`).

### High

- None identified in this repository-only pass.

### Medium

- **Programmatic near-duplicate cluster (investment property).** The flagship `/investment-property-calculator` and **450** indexable state URLs at `/tools/investment-property/{state}` (50 locations × 9 calculator families; investment-property is the overlapping intent) can compete for similar queries. Canonicals are distinct and correct per URL; the risk is **search-quality** (how Google groups or dilutes the set), not a duplicate-canonical error — `app/app/investment-property-calculator/page.tsx`, `app/app/tools/[calculator]/[location]/page.tsx`, `app/lib/marketing/calculator-location-pages.ts` (`basePath` for `investment-property` is `/investment-property-calculator`).
- **Sitemap `lastModified` is still batch-level.** Marketing URLs share `latestChangelogDate()` from `CHANGELOG_ENTRIES` as `lastModified` — better than a static constant, but all non-legal sitemap entries move together; there is no per-URL recency for individual calculator or resource edits unless the changelog (or the implementation) is updated — `app/app/sitemap.ts`, `app/lib/changelog-data.ts` (consumed via import).
- **Environment-dependent sitemap, robots, and metadata.** `getAppOrigin()` in `app/lib/app-url.ts` defaults to `https://veldportfolio.com` when unset, but a wrong `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` on a preview or forked deploy can still emit wrong absolute URLs in `sitemap.xml`, `robots` `Sitemap:`, and `alternates.canonical`. Vercel preview noindex is not confirmed from the codebase; treat as an operational check.

### Low

- **Home page title vs H1 phrasing:** The page title is “Veld Portfolio — Track Your Rental Properties in One Place” while the primary `h1` is “Replace spreadsheet chaos with one clear view.” Both are on-brand, but the keyword alignment between SERP title and the visible headline is not literal — `app/app/page.tsx`.
- **Open Graph and Twitter use the root default image** (`/og-image.png` in `app/app/layout.tsx`) for most child routes. Differentiated social images per major landing or calculator are not set — acceptable for consistency; limits differentiation in rich link previews.
- **Footer internal links (desktop):** The footer links hub pages (`/tools`, `/about`, `/changelog`, `/alternatives`, `/vs`, `/resources`) but not `/pricing` or `/investment-property-calculator`; the landing nav does include `Pricing` and other entry points — `app/components/footer.tsx`, `app/components/landing-nav.tsx`.
- **Stray `learn` route:** `app/app/learn/react-labs/07-timer/page.tsx` is a thin client-only demo. It is **not** in `proxy.ts` public routes, so it requires auth — low external SEO risk, but the route is dead weight in the app tree.
- **Global JSON-LD on every page:** `JsonLdScript` in `app/app/layout.tsx` injects `Organization`, `WebSite`, and `WebApplication` (with offer pricing) on all layouts, including after navigation to authenticated areas. Shell pages are `noindex`; the residual effect on crawlers that ignore `noindex` is minor.

## Risk & impact assessment

Medium findings mostly affect **organic efficiency** and **operational safety** of URLs at scale, not core product security. Misconfigured `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` on a customer-visible environment could cause incorrect canonicals and sitemap listings until fixed — **likelihood** is low for production if deployment checklists are followed; **higher** for previews unless hosting injects `noindex`. Low findings affect snippet nuance, internal discovery paths, and maintainability of stray routes.

## Recommendations (prioritized)

1. **Production and preview verification:** Confirm `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` is `https://veldportfolio.com` (no trailing slash) on production; ensure preview deployments are `noindex` or not linked if policy requires; submit or refresh `sitemap.xml` in Search Console; spot-check **URL Inspection** for `/`, `/investment-property-calculator`, one `/tools/brrr/{state}`, and one `resources/{slug}`.
2. **Programmatic set monitoring:** After deploy, watch Search Console for **duplicate/near-duplicate** hints and query coverage on `/tools/investment-property/*` vs `/investment-property-calculator`; adjust on-page copy, FAQs, or internal links if the grid underperforms or consolidates.
3. **Sitemap `lastModified` depth (optional):** When the team touches specific calculators or resources without a changelog entry, consider moving toward per-segment or build-driven `lastModified` so major URL groups are not all stamped with the same date — lower priority if changelog updates track marketing releases.
