## Executive summary

- Crawl-discovery controls are strong overall: `robots.ts` now disallows major app-only sections, `sitemap.ts` contains the intended marketing/indexable URLs, and LP/contact/auth routes are excluded from sitemap.
- Metadata and title-template handling are improved versus prior runs: root and changelog use absolute titles where appropriate, canonicals are explicit on indexable routes, and OG/Twitter defaults are coherent.
- Structured data coverage is solid for calculator pages (`FAQPage`) plus root site graph (`Organization`, `WebSite`, `WebApplication`) and appears syntactically valid.
- Primary remaining risk is indexability hardening for authenticated pages that still render indexable defaults (no `robots.index: false`) in metadata, relying on auth redirects and `robots.txt` disallow alone.

## Severity-ranked findings

### Critical
- None identified in this repo-based audit pass.

### High
- None identified.

### Medium
- Authenticated app routes are not consistently marked `noindex` in metadata. Example: `/plans` declares metadata but no `robots.index: false`; major app pages like `/dashboard`, `/properties`, `/analyze` have no page metadata override and can inherit indexable defaults if ever served/cached in unexpected contexts. Current mitigation depends on `auth.protect()` plus `robots.txt` disallow, which is good but not as explicit as metadata noindex at app-layout scope. - Evidence: `app/app/(app)/plans/page.tsx`, `app/app/(app)/dashboard/page.tsx`, `app/app/(app)/properties/page.tsx`, `app/app/(app)/analyze/page.tsx`, `app/app/robots.ts`, `app/proxy.ts`

### Low
- Root URL formatting differs between canonical and sitemap (`APP_URL + "/"` vs `APP_URL`). Usually benign, but normalizing root URL shape across canonical/sitemap can reduce edge-case duplicate handling in crawlers/tools. - Evidence: `app/app/page.tsx`, `app/app/sitemap.ts`
- Search Console verification/sitemap submission and staging noindex posture are not verifiable from repository code alone. Operational verification remains pending. - Evidence: repo-only audit limitation

## Risk & impact assessment

- Likelihood of major SEO regression is currently low because sitemap and robots policy are aligned and public pages are well-canonicalized.
- Exposure remains moderate for app-shell URL indexing edge cases if crawlers encounter authenticated routes and metadata does not explicitly noindex at layout/page level.
- Business impact of unresolved medium finding is mainly crawl-budget noise and potential low-quality URL discovery/index attempts rather than ranking loss on primary marketing pages.

---

## Recommendations (prioritized)

1. Add explicit `robots: { index: false, follow: false }` at authenticated app layout level (or consistently per app route) to harden indexability intent beyond redirects/disallow.
2. Keep sitemap/robots/proxy alignment as a release gate whenever public routes are added; ensure new calculator/tool pages are reflected in sitemap and canonicalized.
3. Normalize root URL shape between canonical and sitemap (`/` vs bare origin) for consistency.
