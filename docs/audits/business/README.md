# Business & Valuation Audits

This folder contains business readiness and valuation-oriented audits.

## Process

1. Run: **"run business audit"** or **"run valuation audit"**.
2. Agent follows [docs/process/business-valuation-audit-process.md](../../process/business-valuation-audit-process.md).
3. Reports are written here as `YYYY-MM-DD-business-valuation-audit.md`.
