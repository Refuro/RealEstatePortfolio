# Business & Valuation Audit Process

**Purpose:** Assess product/business readiness, valuation posture, and strategic gaps from an acquirer/operator perspective.

**Status:** Active.

---

## 1. Scope

Audit:

- Revenue and plan architecture readiness
- Differentiation and moat signals
- Risk profile and owner dependence
- Operational/documentation maturity
- Valuation scenarios (codebase-only vs with user traction)

Reference docs:

- `README.md`
- `docs/reference/roadmap.md`
- `docs/business-launch-checklist.md`
- Relevant audits from `docs/audits/`

---

## 2. Audit dimensions

- Product-market fit evidence in current app
- Monetization health and expansion potential
- Strategic defensibility and buyer attractiveness
- Delivery velocity and maintainability signals
- Valuation range assumptions and confidence levels

---

## 3. Output

Write report to:

- `docs/audits/business/YYYY-MM-DD-business-valuation-audit.md`

Use the shared report template in `docs/process/audit-report-template.md`.

---

## 4. Execution

1. Review product, roadmap, and launch/business docs.
2. Assess current state from investor/acquirer lens.
3. Provide valuation framing for no-users and with-users scenarios.
4. Recommend highest-ROI improvements to increase value.
5. Audit only; no code changes.
