# SEO Audit — 2026-04-29

## Executive summary

- **Overall:** Public marketing surfaces are in strong shape: `robots.ts` guards authenticated paths, the authenticated `(app)` layout sets `noindex`, `sitemap.ts` is data-driven and aligned with calculator/competitor/resource slugs, and indexable routes generally define `alternates.canonical` plus `openGraph.url` (relative paths resolved via `metadataBase`).
- **Top risks:** (1) Global JSON-LD `WebApplication` / `Offer` data may be incomplete relative to real billing (monthly-only offers, no annual variants). (2) Homepage `<title>` and on-page H1 emphasize different primary phrases—fine for brand voice but slightly misaligned for query/snippet coherence. (3) Sitemap `lastModified` is largely a single “site changelog” stamp for many URLs, which flattens freshness signals.
- **Recommendation:** Treat SEO hygiene as “maintain and tune”: keep sitemap generators in sync when adding marketing routes, validate structured data in Search Console’s rich-results tests after pricing/schema changes, and optionally refine homepage title/H1 alignment if organic targets for the homepage sharpen.

## Severity-ranked findings

### Critical

- None identified in this static/code pass.

### High

- **Global `WebApplication` + `Offer` JSON-LD may under-represent billing reality** — Incorrect or incomplete offer markup can undermine trust or rich-result eligibility. Root layout (`app/app/layout.tsx`) embeds `Offer` objects with monthly amounts from `PRICING_DISPLAY` only (no annual prices, no explicit billing period in schema). If production prices or intervals diverge from env defaults, structured data can drift from the pricing page without anyone noticing.

### Medium

- **Homepage `<title>` vs H1 primary wording diverge** — Weaker alignment between SERP title and first heading can modestly affect relevance signals and user expectation. `app/app/page.tsx`: title centers “Track Your Rental Properties in One Place”; H1 is “Replace spreadsheet chaos with one clear view.” (Both are defensible; harmonizing is optional unless targeting specific keywords.)
- **Sitemap `lastModified` is coarse for large programmatic sets** — Crawlers get weaker per-URL freshness hints. `app/app/sitemap.ts` sets `SITE_LAST_MODIFIED` from latest changelog date for most marketing URLs (including all state/calculator combos). Individual resource or calculator page edits don’t bump URLs unless changelog or manual legal date changes.
- **Operational: staging / preview indexability** — Not determined from repo. Preview deployments should be `noindex` or blocked; confirm in Vercel/project settings and spot-check a preview URL’s `X-Robots-Tag` / meta robots live.

### Low

- **Footer marketing links are desktop-priority in one row** — `app/components/footer.tsx` hides the Calculators/About/Changelog/Alternatives/Compare/Resources row under `md:`. Mobile users still get these links via `landing-nav.tsx` menu, so crawl paths exist; the footer pattern is slightly asymmetric for “bottom of page” internal linking on small viewports.
- **`/llms.txt` is public (proxy allowlist) but not sitemap-listed** — Expected for LLM discovery; no issue unless you want it explicitly discoverable via Search Console sitemap (usually unnecessary).
- **Internationalization** — Single locale (`lang="en"` on `<html>`); `hreflang` N/A.

## Risk & impact assessment

Unresolved **High** structured-data gaps are unlikely to crash rankings overnight but can cap rich-result wins and create compliance risk if offers are read literally by crawlers. **Medium** items skew toward crawl efficiency and messaging alignment rather than hard penalties. Exposure is primarily to organic discovery and share-preview quality on marketing URLs.

## Recommendations (prioritized)

1. **Structured data:** Revisit root `WebApplication` / `Offer` markup when pricing changes: consider adding annual offers, clarifying subscription terms, or narrowing schema to types you can keep provably in sync with Stripe and the pricing page (may mean simplifying offers in JSON-LD).
2. **Sitemap freshness:** When a high-value URL changes outside the changelog (e.g. a pillar resource article), bump its `lastModified` intentionally—either per-URL in `sitemap.ts` or by tying to content file/version metadata.
3. **Homepage:** If SEO experiments target landlord “portfolio tracking” head terms, test a title/H1 pairing that shares one primary phrase without hurting conversion copy.
